# CLAUDE.md

## Frontend design

- Always use the `design-taste-frontend` skill when working on frontend design or visual UI: layouts, typography, spacing, colors, components, landing pages, article layouts, navigation, or redesigns.
- Do not use it for backend or other non-design work.
- If it conflicts with TGK-specific design rules or the `tgk-working-rules` skill, the TGK rules take priority.
